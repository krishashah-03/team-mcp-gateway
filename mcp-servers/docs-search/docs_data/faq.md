# Frequently Asked Questions

## Which payment methods do you accept?
Cards, UPI and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is limited to orders below 10000.

## Can I change my order after placing it?
Yes, within one hour of placing it, while the status is **processing**. Email support
with the order number. After the order is shipped it can no longer be changed.

## How do I know my order status?
Statuses are: pending (awaiting payment), processing (being packed), on-hold, completed
(delivered), cancelled, refunded and failed.
