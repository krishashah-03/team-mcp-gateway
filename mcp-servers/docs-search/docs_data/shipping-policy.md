# Shipping Policy

We ship Monday to Saturday. Orders placed before 2 PM are dispatched the same day.

## Delivery times
- Standard delivery: 3 to 5 business days.
- Express delivery: 1 to 2 business days, available for orders above 999.
- Free standard shipping on orders above 1499.

## Tracking
A tracking link is emailed as soon as the order is handed to the courier. If an order
is still **pending** after 24 hours, contact support so we can check the payment.
