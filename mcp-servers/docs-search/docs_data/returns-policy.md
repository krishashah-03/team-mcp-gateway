# Returns and Refunds Policy

Customers may return any unused item within **30 days** of delivery for a full refund.

## How to return
1. Email support with the order number and the reason for the return.
2. We reply within one business day with a prepaid return label.
3. Once the parcel is received and inspected, the refund is issued to the original
   payment method within 5 business days.

## Exceptions
- Gift cards and digital downloads cannot be returned.
- Items damaged by the customer are not eligible for a refund.
- Orders paid by cash on delivery are refunded by bank transfer.
